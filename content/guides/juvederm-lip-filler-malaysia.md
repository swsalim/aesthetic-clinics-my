## What to Check Before Enquiring About Juvederm Filler

A clear enquiry prevents a vague quote from becoming an unexpected bill or an unsuitable appointment. Ask the clinic to confirm:

- Which Juvederm product the doctor proposes and whether it is intended for the requested area.
- Whether the doctor will assess you before deciding on any procedure.
- The total quoted fee, including consultation, procedure-related charges and follow-up arrangements.
- Which risks and possible complications the doctor will explain during consultation.
- What happens if you need to reschedule or the clinic cannot provide the requested product.

Do not treat a product name alone as proof that it suits every person or every area. The United States Food and Drug Administration describes JUVÉDERM VOLBELLA XC as a temporary dermal or soft-tissue filler with specific approved uses; product labelling and local clinical assessment still matter. Ask who will assess and perform the procedure. The answer should identify the treating doctor, rather than only naming the clinic or sales consultant.

### When not to book a lip filler consultation

Pause the enquiry if the provider won’t identify the treating doctor, refuses to explain the total fee, cannot clarify the product being offered, or pressures you to pay before a consultation. A directory does not provide medical advice, and personal suitability requires assessment by a qualified practitioner.

A low advertised price cannot answer the clinical questions that determine whether an appointment is appropriate.

## Credentials and Regulation to Verify

Malaysia’s Ministry of Health tells the public to check three areas before an aesthetic procedure: the procedure, the practitioner and the premises. Its official [aesthetic medical practice guidance](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/estetik/) states that an aesthetic practitioner should be a registered medical practitioner with a Letter of Credentialing and Privileging, or LCP, and that the premises must be registered with the Ministry of Health.

Ask the clinic for the treating doctor’s full name, Malaysian Medical Council registration details and relevant LCP information. Verify registration through the [Malaysian Medical Council MeRITS search](https://merits.mmc.gov.my/search) and check the Ministry of Health’s [National Registry of RMP Practising Aesthetic Medical Practice](https://www.moh.gov.my/en/core/health-information/national-registry-of-rmp-practising-aesthetic-medical-practice), using the current official records.

Do not infer LCP status from a directory badge, clinic advertisement or search result. Ask the clinic to explain which doctor holds the relevant credential and verify the claim before booking. The Malaysia Medical Device Register can also be searched for device or brand information, but a registered product does not prove that a particular clinic or doctor is suitable.
