# Does the site give medical advice?

No. The directory and its guides are general information, not medical advice, and they do not promise treatment results. For diagnosis, suitability and prices, consult a registered medical practitioner and check their LCP status with Malaysia's Ministry of Health. See the [LCP comparison guide](../articles/compare-lcp-certified-clinics.md).
